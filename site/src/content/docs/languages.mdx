## Choose a model that covers your language

Parakeet v3 covers 25 European languages. The optional shared All languages
package uses Whisper turbo for additional supported speech languages. These
choices describe recognition coverage, not translated app UI or a guarantee for
every accent, dialect or mixed-language passage.

The nineteen linked language guides are listed alphabetically by English display
name. Settings includes the model's other available language choices as well.

## Auto or one to three pins

In Settings → Languages, use Auto or pin up to three languages. Enable automatic
model choice to route those pins to a compatible installed model, or explicitly
select a compatible model yourself. The controls do not start a download.

A single Whisper pin supplies its decoder language; multiple pins leave language
detection enabled. Parakeet does not accept that same explicit decoder hint.
Automatic model routing and language detection can be imperfect, especially for
short, quiet or mixed-language speech. Review your results and language choice.

## One shared multilingual download

Arabic, Bengali, Chinese, Filipino / Tagalog, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese,
Korean, Malay, Persian, Tamil, Telugu, Thai, Urdu and Vietnamese use the shared
All languages route. French, German, Portuguese and Spanish are also in Parakeet
v3's coverage. Installing another language choice reuses the model's weights;
there are no per-language download buttons.

See <SiteLink path="docs/models/">model sizes and explicit setup</SiteLink>.

## Portuguese and Chinese details

Portuguese uses **pt**, including Brazil. The current automatic route is based
on coverage, not a verified preference for every Brazilian accent. NVIDIA notes
European Portuguese training in its original model card. Compare representative
speech before choosing a model for your workflow.

Mandarin Chinese uses **zh**. Cantonese, **yue**, is a separate menu choice using
the multilingual weights. The app does not expose a Simplified/Traditional
conversion setting or establish coverage of every Chinese dialect.

## Coverage and measured accuracy

The public 20-language evaluation reveals substantial errors in Bengali, Tamil
and Telugu. Larger decoder candidates have not established a general improvement.
Broad owner accuracy remains deferred. Review names, numbers and technical terms
before sending a draft; text cleanup does not prove that recognition was correct.

Dictation language, the Style preview language and experimental Translate output
targets are separate choices. Current spoken Translate targets are English,
Spanish and Arabic. See <SiteLink path="docs/styles/">styles and translation</SiteLink>.
