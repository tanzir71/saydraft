## Add the spelling you want

Open Hub → Dictionary to add people, places and specialist terms. Protected
terms help retain exact wording through cleanup. Use an explicit replacement
pair when a particular recognized phrase should become another phrase, rather
than expecting a fuzzy match to infer your intention.

Dictionary, replacement and protected-term edits are staged until Save. The
<SiteLink path="docs/styles/">Style preview</SiteLink> includes those pending
edits without writing them to disk. Fix an invalid draft before saving.

## Understand spelling correction

Normal resolved English dictation can use conservative phonetic and spelling
evidence to correct close matches to dictionary names, without another ASR pass.
Ambiguous targets, common words, numbers, code, identifiers and protected/literal
text decline fuzzy changes. Explicit replacement pairs take priority.

Raw, Verbatim-ish, Code, translation, unknown or other languages and mixed scripts
do not receive these English phonetic guesses. Longer unsupported entries retain
their exact protection rather than being silently shortened. History retains the
original recognized text alongside the processed result.

The public evaluation covers a limited English name set; it does not establish
general or owner accuracy. See the linked investigation for its method and limits.

## Choose correction suggestions

**Suggest words from corrections** is experimental and off by default. When
enabled, local observation of the original editable field can identify a narrow
word correction after a dictation. The suggestion offers **Add** or **Dismiss**.
It does not enable a cloud learning service.

**Auto-add to dictionary** is a separate, default-off choice available with
suggestions. It saves eligible additions automatically and offers Undo after a
successful save. Keep Auto-add off if you prefer to approve suggestions yourself.
Native/manual behavior is still being qualified.

## Undo and conflicting edits

Undo belongs to that saved addition. It preserves unrelated dictionary changes
and can decline when a newer/manual change has claimed the same term. It does
not undo the words you edited in another app.

An edited stale dictionary draft is refused before shared settings are saved.
Keep your draft, then use Reload Dictionary to review the latest saved version
before trying again. Invalid data are not replaced with an empty dictionary.

## Keep the privacy boundary clear

Suggestions involve optional local field observation. Audio retention and
Command Mode's clipboard fallback are separate settings. Read
<SiteLink path="privacy/">privacy</SiteLink> for those boundaries, and use
<SiteLink path="docs/troubleshooting/">troubleshooting</SiteLink> if saving or
recognition behaves unexpectedly.
