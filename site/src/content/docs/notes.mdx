## Open Scratchpad

Press **Win + Alt + S**, or choose **New note** in Hub → Notes. Scratchpad is a
resizable plain-text window. Opening it starts no recording. You can type without
an AI model, or dictate into its focused field using the normal shortcut and
compatible installed speech models.

## Save a draft locally

A draft saves after a short typing pause. Closing the window or moving between
notes waits for pending edits. An untouched blank draft creates no note.
Reopening Scratchpad resumes its last saved draft, including after a later app
launch. The draft and saved notes stay in the local app data.

If saving fails, the window keeps your unsaved text and offers recovery. A
revision conflict refuses to overwrite another edit; **Save as new** preserves
your draft separately. Review any discard confirmation before dropping text.

## Review saved Notes

Hub → Notes provides search and plain-text review. Scratchpad, Command, Ask and
meeting results are independent note origins. Creating a meeting Note does not
replace the active Scratchpad. Notes browsing does not record, inspect another
app or need a network connection.

Copy uses the app's Windows clipboard privacy handling. Windows clipboard
history or sync can have separate behavior; read the
<SiteLink path="privacy/">privacy boundaries</SiteLink> before copying sensitive text.

## Experimental meeting notes

Meeting notes are separate from ordinary Scratchpad. They require the Pro
hardware profile, an explicit Labs opt-in, reviewed model downloads, selected
inputs and participant permission. Enabling the feature or finishing a model
download never starts recording.

The current source provides local recording/analysis controls and transcript
review. Its complete live/frozen workflow, a 30-minute meeting and broad speech,
speaker-label and summary accuracy are still unaccepted. Review names, decisions
and task owners against the transcript. Do not treat generated notes as a
verified record without that review.

See the linked implementation guide for current setup and recovery details,
<SiteLink path="docs/models/">model requirements</SiteLink> for dependencies, and
<SiteLink path="docs/shortcuts/">shortcuts</SiteLink> for rebinding.
