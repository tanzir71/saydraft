# Wispr Flow Alternative for Windows, Offline | Saydraft

Canonical: https://tanzir71.github.io/saydraft/wispr-flow-alternative/

Compare Saydraft with Wispr Flow for Windows dictation: local models, cloud processing, pricing and setup. Free, open-source voice typing.

Facts checked: 2026-11-14

If you want voice typing that continues after you disconnect, Saydraft offers a local route on Windows. Speech models and optional text cleanup run on your PC. The project is free and MIT licensed. An unsigned Windows portable preview is available from the [download page](https://tanzir71.github.io/saydraft/download/), alongside source setup. Review the preview limits before relying on it for daily work.

 

Wispr Flow is a commercial dictation service for Windows and macOS. Its [documentation](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/2772472373-what-is-flow) describes cloud transcription. Local transcript history and privacy preferences do not make that transcription an offline service.

Wispr currently lists Pro at US$15 per user monthly, or US$144 per user annually. Its free plan has a weekly word allowance; check the pricing page for the current limit and billing terms. Saydraft has no subscription. Its Pro setting describes a local capability tier, rather than a paid plan.

 

## Moving a writing workflow

 

- Follow the [setup guide](https://tanzir71.github.io/saydraft/docs/getting-started/) and explicitly install a compatible speech model.
 
- Start with a plain text editor. Hold Ctrl+Win, speak a short passage, then release. Shortcuts can be changed in Settings.
 
- Pin the [languages you use](https://tanzir71.github.io/saydraft/languages/), add names to your dictionary and review the transcript before sending it elsewhere.

 

These are familiar push-to-talk gestures; they do not import Wispr’s settings, account or vocabulary. Avoid running overlapping shortcuts in two dictation apps at once. Recognition and cleanup differ, so compare the output on your own words rather than assuming equal accuracy.

## A few useful answers.

### Does Wispr Flow work without internet?

Wispr documents cloud transcription and an internet requirement. Saydraft processes speech with installed local models, which can be reused offline.

### Is Saydraft ready to replace my current dictation app?

Saydraft has an unsigned Windows portable preview and MIT-licensed source. Try it with your own vocabulary and apps first; broader accuracy and native Windows workflow acceptance remain in progress.
