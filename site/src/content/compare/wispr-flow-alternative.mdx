If you want voice typing that continues after you disconnect, Saydraft offers a
local route on Windows. Speech models and optional text cleanup run on your PC.
The project is free and MIT licensed, with its current implementation available
from source.

Wispr Flow is a commercial dictation service for Windows and macOS. Its
[documentation](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/2772472373-what-is-flow)
describes cloud transcription. Local transcript history and privacy preferences
do not make that transcription an offline service.

## Compare the trade-offs

| Choice | Saydraft | Wispr Flow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speech processing | [Installed local models](https://github.com/tanzir71/saydraft/blob/main/docs/MODELS.md) | [Cloud transcription](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/2772472373-what-is-flow) |
| Desktop platform | [Windows](https://github.com/tanzir71/saydraft) | [Windows and macOS](https://wisprflow.ai/pricing) |
| Cost | [Free, MIT licensed source](https://github.com/tanzir71/saydraft/blob/main/LICENSE) | [Free allowance; paid Pro](https://wisprflow.ai/pricing) |
| Current availability | [Development source; release qualification continues](https://github.com/tanzir71/saydraft) | [Commercial product](https://wisprflow.ai/pricing) |

Wispr currently lists Pro at US$15 per user monthly, or US$144 per user annually.
Its free plan has a weekly word allowance; check the pricing page for the
current limit and billing terms. Saydraft has no subscription. Its Pro setting
describes a local capability tier, rather than a paid plan.

## Moving a writing workflow

1. Follow the <SiteLink path="docs/getting-started/">setup guide</SiteLink> and
   explicitly install a compatible speech model.
2. Start with a plain text editor. Hold Ctrl+Win, speak a short passage, then
   release. Shortcuts can be changed in Settings.
3. Pin the <SiteLink path="languages/">languages you use</SiteLink>, add names
   to your dictionary and review the transcript before sending it elsewhere.

These are familiar push-to-talk gestures; they do not import Wispr's settings,
account or vocabulary. Avoid running overlapping shortcuts in two dictation
apps at once. Recognition and cleanup differ, so compare the output on your own
words rather than assuming equal accuracy.
