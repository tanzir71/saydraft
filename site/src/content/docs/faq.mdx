faqs:
  - q: "Is Saydraft free?"
    a: "Yes. Saydraft's own code is free and open source under MIT. Models, fonts, libraries and adapted data retain their separate licenses."
  - q: "Do I need an account or subscription?"
    a: "No. The app uses local models without an account. Lite, Standard and Pro describe local hardware/performance profiles, not paid plans."
  - q: "Can I dictate without internet?"
    a: "Yes, with compatible models installed first. Download setup is explicit and optional update checks are off by default. Missing model files are not fetched during recognition."
  - q: "Are Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese included?"
    a: "Both are covered by Parakeet v3 and the shared All languages model. Portuguese uses pt, including Brazil; this is coverage rather than a claim of verified accuracy for every accent."
  - q: "What about Arabic, Bengali, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Thai?"
    a: "They use the optional shared All languages model. Filipino/Tagalog, Hindi, Indonesian, Malay, Persian, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu and Vietnamese also use that shared route. There are no per-language packs."
  - q: "Will it get every word right?"
    a: "No. Accuracy varies by language, model, accent and recording conditions. The public baseline exposes substantial Bengali, Tamil and Telugu errors. Review names, numbers and technical terms before using a draft."
  - q: "Do I need an AI cleanup model?"
    a: "No. Deterministic cleanup rules work without a text model. Optional local AI needs its own model/runtime and can retain the rules result when unready, out of budget or rejected."
  - q: "Is Whisper mode a different speech model?"
    a: "No. The experimental Microphone setting boosts quiet speech with gain, AGC and optional local enhancement. It works before the selected recognizer; its whispered-speech accuracy target remains unmeasured."
  - q: "Are recordings kept?"
    a: "Audio retention is off by default. History stores recognized and processed text locally; choosing to retain audio is separate. Practice in setup stays in memory and creates no History entry."
  - q: "Does the website listen to me?"
    a: "No. Its examples are canned visual simulations with no microphone access, tracking analytics or third-party font requests."
